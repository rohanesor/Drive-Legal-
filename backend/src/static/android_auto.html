Android Auto Integration

Bring Vazhi to Your Vehicle Display

● Available in Android App

Android Auto is built directly into the official Vazhi Android application via VazhiCarAppService.

Setup Instructions:
1. Install the Vazhi Android APK on your smartphone.
2. Connect your phone to your car using USB cable or Wireless Android Auto.
3. Select Vazhi Navigation from your car's Android Auto launcher.
