Setup Instructions

  1. Download and install the latest DriveLegal Android APK on your phone.
  2. Open the app and switch to Car Mode from the navigation menu or bottom bar.
  3. Mount your phone in a dashboard cradle for high-visibility hands-free HUD interface.
  4. Use voice controls or big-button touch controls to ask traffic legal questions while driving.

Car Mode Features

  • Hands-Free Voice Controls: Built-in native speech recognition and Text-To-Speech for minimum distraction.
  • Predictive Zone Alerts: Automatic audio alerts when approaching high-risk zones, speed limit changes, or school zones.
